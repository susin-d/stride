# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

High-agency professionals, engineers, designers, founders, and knowledge workers who juggle complex projects, deep focus sessions, and daily habits. They seek clarity and momentum over bloated project management tools.

## Product Purpose

STRIDE exists to help ambitious individuals plan intentionally, execute with deep focus, and maintain consistent daily momentum across projects, goals, and habits. Success means closing the day with visible forward progress and zero administrative friction.

## Positioning

Unlike complex enterprise task trackers that feel like spreadsheets or simplistic to-do lists that lack habit and focus context, STRIDE integrates task planning, goal hierarchy, habit streaks, and backend-anchored deep work timers into a unified, calm, tactile command center.

## Operating Context

Daily morning planning, mid-day deep focus sprints, and evening ritual reviews. Used primarily on desktop and laptop browsers during high-focus work sessions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Task management with priorities, due dates, project association, and subtasks
- Goal tracking with milestone progress
- Daily habit tracking with monthly completion heatmaps and streak counts
- Backend-anchored Pomodoro and deep work timer safe against browser sleep
- Weekly review and productivity analytics
- Fast responsive web application built with React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and FastAPI

## Brand Commitments

- Name: STRIDE
- Core tagline: "Plan Better. Move Forward."
- Voice: Measured, articulate, confident, and direct. No hustle-culture hyperbole, no playful cartoonishness.
- Aesthetic: High-craft, tactile neumorphic-subtle editorial design with deep ink black, calm surfaces, and micro-precision typography.

## Product Principles

1. **Velocity without noise:** Every interaction is immediate, legible, and unburdened by unnecessary modals or configuration.
2. **Integrated momentum:** Habits, tasks, goals, and focus timers reinforce each other rather than living in isolated silos.
3. **Calm authority:** The visual interface recedes so the user's priorities and focus remain front and center.
4. **Fidelity to craft:** Refined typography, tactile depth, and responsive states across all viewports.
