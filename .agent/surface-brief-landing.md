# Surface Brief: Landing Page

Scope: Marketing & visitor conversion surface
Mode: Persuade

## Target
frontend/src/pages/LandingPage.tsx

## Direction contract
THESIS: High-craft editorial showcase demonstrating STRIDE's clarity, tactile precision, and unified productivity engine, refusing generic SaaS marketing clichés.
OWN-WORLD: Deep neutral palette (#F1F1F3 ground, #111111 ink, crisp warm white surfaces, refined dark contrast), tactile depth, balanced sans typography, micro-precision borders.
STORY: Visitors discover how STRIDE harmonizes task planning, deep work timers, habit tracking, and quarterly goals into a quiet, friction-free workflow, interact with a live interactive simulation of the app, and take immediate action.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Asymmetrical editorial header with brand mark, manifesto headline "Plan Better. Move Forward.", succinct framing, "Get Started Free" primary CTA and "Sign In" secondary CTA, followed by an interactive multi-tab live preview (Today's Tasks, Pomodoro Focus Timer, Habit Streak Heatmap, and Goal Milestones).
FORM: Editorial product presentation with live interactive components and fluid responsive layouts.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
